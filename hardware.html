Hardware

This section highlights the physical design and mechanical implementation of the Smart Sorting Trash Can, including CAD modeling, structural layout, and embedded electronics.

CAD Design and Mechanical Structure

The system was designed using CAD software to model both the external enclosure and internal sorting mechanism. The design includes a top intake chamber that directs waste onto a hinged platform capable of rotating about a central axis.

Parametric modeling was used to ensure proper alignment, clearances, and smooth actuation of the platform. Iterative adjustments were made to optimize fit and functionality prior to fabrication.

Embedded Electronics and Layout

The electronic system is centered around a Raspberry Pi, which interfaces with sensing, imaging, and actuation components. Two high-torque servo motors are mounted at the base of the hinged platform and controlled via PWM signals to enable precise rotation.

An infrared sensor is positioned at the intake to detect object placement, while a camera mounted on the lid captures images for classification. This arrangement ensures proper coordination between detection, imaging, and mechanical response.
